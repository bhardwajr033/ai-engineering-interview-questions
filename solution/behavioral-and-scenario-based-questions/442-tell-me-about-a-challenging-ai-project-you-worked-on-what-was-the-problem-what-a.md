# Tell me about a challenging AI project you worked on. What was the problem? What approach did you take? What trade-offs did you make? What was the outcome?

**Topic:** Behavioral and Scenario-Based Questions

## Interview Answer

A strong behavioral answer should show judgment, measurement, communication, and ownership rather than only naming AI techniques.

Key points:
- Use a STAR structure: situation, task, action, result.
- Explain the baseline, why it failed, and what evidence led to your chosen approach.
- Call out trade-offs such as latency vs quality, RAG vs fine-tuning, or automation vs human review.
- Quantify the outcome with quality, cost, latency, adoption, or incident metrics.

## Example

Example answer shape: `We started with a rules/RAG baseline, measured failure cases, added evaluation, improved retrieval, and shipped behind a feature flag with monitoring`.

## Current Software Engineering Use Case

Used by software teams building behavioral and scenario-based questions features that must balance quality, latency, cost, safety, and maintainability.
