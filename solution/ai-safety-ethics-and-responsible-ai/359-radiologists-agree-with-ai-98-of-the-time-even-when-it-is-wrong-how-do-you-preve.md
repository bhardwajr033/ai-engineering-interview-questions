# Radiologists agree with AI 98% of the time, even when it is wrong. How do you prevent human over-reliance on AI?

**Topic:** AI Safety, Ethics, and Responsible AI

## Responsible AI

Responsible AI applies controls so AI systems are useful while respecting safety, privacy, fairness, transparency, and accountability.

Key points:
- Controls include data minimization, PII redaction, access control, bias testing, explainability, audit logs, human review, and incident response.
- High-risk domains require stronger validation, documentation, monitoring, and appeal mechanisms.
- Security threats include prompt injection, data poisoning, model backdoors, misuse, and leakage of sensitive information.

## Example

Example: a loan assistant should log evidence, provide adverse-action explanations, avoid protected-attribute proxies, and support human appeal.

## Current Software Engineering Use Case

A hiring, healthcare, finance, or education AI system needs audit trails, fairness evaluation, human escalation, and clear user disclosures before deployment.
